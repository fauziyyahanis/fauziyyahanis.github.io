GoDentist Enhancement 2025

Product Management Problem Identification Wireframe Proposed Solution Prioritization Product Discovery

This is my final project for Product Management Bootcamp at dibimbing.id. I had the opportunity to do a one-month internship at GoDentist for my final project, allowing me to implement the knowledge I gained during the bootcamp.

Overview

GoDentist is a tech startup that provides accessible dental and oral health services through technology.

Focused on increasing the number of active users of the GoDentist mobile app, I started by identifying pain points across the user journey while using the main features. I categorized the pain points into three problem categories:

  1. Booking process consideration phase
  2. Booking process activation phase
  3. Online chat consultation issue

For the booking process consideration phase, I emphasized providing clear, concise, and important information to patients. This helps patients better understand the features and make informed decisions with greater confidence.

In the booking process activation phase, I focused on providing a logical flow and relevant information to be filled out by the patient in the booking form. This improves the user experience by reducing booking errors and form completion time while ensuring patients provide accurate and necessary details.

For the online chat problem, I improved the flow to be fairer for patients while keeping it convenient for both patients and dentists. This aims to increase patient satisfaction and reduce complaints about the online chat consultation feature, ultimately supporting more chat consultations for dentists.
