Background

Kitabisa as a crowdfunding platform in Indonesia with at least 8 million users, saw the potential to expand its services into Sharia life insurance with a product called SalingJaga, which follows the concept of mutual help through membership fund collection. However, Kitabisa faced the challenge of a low conversion rate from crowdfunding users to Sharia life insurance users.

Market Sizing & OKR

Before jumping in into problem identification, I try to understand the business by identifying the market and creating an OKR. Customer needs/Paint Points:

  • Feel the need of financially secure for the family in the time after the user’s death.
  • Doubt in using conventional life insurance considering their transaction is not based on sharia law transaction

Value Proposition: Providing easy-access financial security by giving the amount of compensation to the family left behind by mutual help through a community collective fund that aligned with Sharia law. Ideal Customer Profile: Kitabisa donors aged 17-60 years who regularly make donations at least once a month.
